# Batch 4-6 decisions sheet — out-of-GT review rows (queue rows 61-118)

**Counts:** 32 RECOVERED / 19 DEGRADED reviewed here (51 queue rows, 48 crops); **7 NOT_TEXT excluded** (all page-111 index furniture — dot leaders and page-number fragments, being closed separately).

**Scope and the rule.** These rows sit on pages 68-111, past the page-67 end of ground truth, so every one is `out_of_gt_scope`: there is no gold verse to confirm it against. A human reading the source-image crop is therefore the **only** path to a `full_line` record for these rows. This sheet registers nothing and changes no data — it prepares your decisions so you can sit down and close rows one crop at a time (`data/reviewed_corrections.jsonl` is untouched).

**Machine readings are candidates, not confirmations — provenance note.** Everything in the reading columns below — PaddleOCR *and* Gemini alike — is a candidate that has to be checked against the crop image. **You, looking at the crop, are the only authority.** There is no ground truth to fall back on here, so no reading on this sheet can be confirmed by matching a gold verse. A vision LLM can hallucinate fluent, plausible, correctly-diacritized Arabic: the project's own probe (`logs/gemini_crossread_plan.md`) caught OpenRouter's `llama-4-scout-17b` returning a **different Quranic verse for every crop** regardless of what the image showed (p088_02 → «لا تقف ما ليس لك به علم», p087_09 → «لا إكراه في الدين») — it was generating from an Arabic religious-text prior, not reading the page. That failure mode is exactly why the Gemini cross-read uses two independent keys: agreement *between* them is evidence, and a single confident reading is not. **Be most suspicious of high-confidence readings on degraded crops** — Section D is where a fluent hallucination is hardest to catch, because the PaddleOCR there gives you nothing to compare it against.

**Verdict legend — what the `verdict` column means** (values are filled in from `logs/gemini_crossread.jsonl` once the cross-read run finishes):

- **AGREE** — both Gemini readings match at the skeleton level (high `skeleton_agreement`: same letters, same order, diacritics stripped and spaces removed). The reading is a **strong candidate**; a human still has to look at the crop before it is confirmed.
- **MINOR** — the two readings agree on the letter skeleton and diverge only on diacritics. **Adopt the letters, flag the diacritics** as unconfirmed.
- **DISAGREE** — the two readings diverge materially (different words, a different line, or one reading much shorter than the other). **No candidate — a human must read the crop.**

**A Gemini reading is NOT a `full_line` confirmation.** `full_line` means a person read and confirmed the line; a vision LLM reading is not a person. Per the project's core rule (`logs/gemini_crossread_plan.md`), a Gemini reading becomes a **`partial`** record carrying provenance — `source: "gemini_crossread"`, with both readings and the agreement score named in the note, the same standard applied to batch 2-3's programmatic GT matching. **Only human eyes on the crop image upgrade a row to `full_line`.** That also means every Gemini-derived record can be isolated and reverted as a set if Gemini turns out to have a bias on this text.

**The Gemini columns are placeholders.** `Gemini 1`, `Gemini 2`, `agreement`, and `verdict` read `—` (pending) on every row. A parallel agent is running both Gemini keys over all 48 crops into `logs/gemini_crossread.jsonl` (fields: `crop, page, region, key1, key2, skeleton_agreement, status`); those four cells are filled from that file when the run finishes. **No Gemini reading or agreement score on this sheet is invented** — every `—` means "not yet run", not "no text". Long Arabic OCR strings are elided with `…` to keep the rows readable; the full untruncated text is in `logs/batch456_triage.md`. The **ACTION column is never truncated.**

**Be honest about the OCR.** Every PaddleOCR reading below is `arabic_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec` run on a narrow crop band. It merges adjacent words, drops diacritics, splits words randomly, and sometimes hallucinates. Confidence is **not** accuracy: four DEGRADED rows score 0.65-0.69 because the OCR confidently produced text that ties to no line of the page, and the two cleanest readings in the whole batch (conf 0.79 and 0.84) are prose that is not the verse the queue asked about. Where a note says MARGINAL, SUSPECT, or low-confidence, treat the OCR as a hint, not a reading. **You are the authority; the OCR is a starting point.** This model cannot view images, so nothing here has been visually verified — and Gemini's confidence is the same trap in a different costume, so apply the same standard to its columns.

**Read these first — ordering.** Sections are ordered by how fast you can close them: the **four mis-paired crops** first (each has a clean crop OCR that already identifies the true line, and between them they fix 6 queue rows — you only have to reject the stored guess and confirm what the crop shows); then the **clean, complete recoveries** by OCR confidence (quick confirms, same line as the guess); then the **marginal, short, and suspect-pairing** recoveries (plausible but needs your eyes before adopting); and the **19 DEGRADED rows last**, grouped at the end, where the OCR contributes nothing and you decipher the image from scratch. The Gemini verdict does not change this order — it just sorts *within* it: once the cross-read lands, the AGREE and MINOR rows in each section are the ones you can close at a glance, and the DISAGREE rows go to the back of that section.
